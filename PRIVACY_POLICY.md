# Privacy Policy — HabitFlow

**Last updated:** 23 August 2026

HabitFlow (`com.riddhiman.habitflow`) is an offline habit tracker. This policy
describes what the app does with your information. The short version: it keeps
everything on your device and sends nothing anywhere.

## What we collect

**Nothing.** HabitFlow has no servers, no accounts and no analytics. The app
makes no network requests of any kind.

## What is stored on your device

All of the following is written to your device's private app storage and never
leaves it:

- The habits you create — title, icon, colour, schedule, daily goal and note.
- The days you have marked each habit complete.
- Your preferences — theme, accent colour, first day of the week, reminder and
  haptics settings.

No other app can read this data. It is removed when you uninstall HabitFlow.

## Permissions

| Permission | Why |
|---|---|
| `POST_NOTIFICATIONS` | To show the habit reminders you schedule yourself. Declining it disables reminders and nothing else. |
| `RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED` | To restore your reminders after the device restarts. Android clears scheduled alarms on reboot. |
| `VIBRATE` | The short buzz that accompanies a reminder. |

HabitFlow does not request access to your location, contacts, camera,
microphone, files, or any other sensitive data.

## Sharing

Your data is never shared with anyone, including us.

The **Back up data** feature copies your habits to your clipboard, and only when
you explicitly ask it to. What you then do with that text — save it, send it to
another device, paste it somewhere — is entirely your choice and outside the
app's control.

## Deleting your data

- **Settings › Delete all data** erases every habit and all history immediately.
- Uninstalling HabitFlow removes everything the app has stored.

There is nothing for us to delete on your behalf, because we never receive
anything.

## Children

HabitFlow is suitable for all ages. Because it collects no data at all, it
collects no data from children either.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at the same URL
and the date at the top will change. Since the app collects nothing, any change
is likely to be a clarification rather than a new practice.
